Last updated: October 2026

ZamGig may collect information that users provide when creating an account or using the platform, including their name, phone number, email address, location, profile information and information relating to gigs and services.

We use information to provide and operate ZamGig, connect users with opportunities, manage accounts, process platform activities, provide notifications, improve the service and maintain platform security.

3. Profile Information

Users may provide profile photographs, skills, availability and other information. Some profile information may be visible to other ZamGig users when necessary to provide the platform's services.

4. Communication

ZamGig may process communications and platform activity where necessary to operate the service, investigate reports, prevent abuse and protect users and the platform.

5. Payments and Wallet Information

Where wallet or payment-related features are used, relevant transaction and account information may be recorded for platform administration, verification and accounting purposes.

6. Security

ZamGig takes reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third Parties

ZamGig may use service providers and infrastructure providers necessary to host, operate and maintain the platform.

8. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for providing accurate information and protecting their account credentials. Users should notify ZamGig if they believe their account has been compromised.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of ZamGig after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, users may contact ZamGig through the contact information provided on the platform.
